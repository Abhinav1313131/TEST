STEP 01

Save the file

Hit Save skin PNG. You get a 64×64 PNG with transparency intact — the transparent pixels are the second layer, which is what gives the hair its volume. Never re-save it as JPG or flatten it.

STEP 02

Upload to your profile

In the Minecraft Launcher open the Skins tab → New skin → browse to the PNG → set the model to Classic (wide arms) → Save. The web route is the same file: your Minecraft account page → Skins → Browse → Upload.

STEP 03

Join the realm

The skin lives on your Microsoft account, not on the realm, so it follows you into every world the moment the game restarts. If a friend still sees the old one, they just need to rejoin — their client caches skins per session.
